FAQ

Questions teams ask before they install.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@trails.dev and a product person replies the same business day.

How long until I see my first drop-off?

Most teams see a ranked journey map within a day of installing. You need roughly 200 sign-ups for the numbers to be dependable, so lower-volume products may take a week.

We already use Mixpanel or Amplitude. Why add Trails?

Those tools answer the questions you already know to ask. Trails finds the step you didn't know was broken and shows the errors and behavior behind it. Many customers keep both and send Trails data to their existing tools.

Will the script slow down my app?

The script is 14 KB gzipped, loads asynchronously and batches events, so it doesn't block rendering or your own API calls.

Do I need an engineer to set it up?

Only to paste one script tag, or not at all if you already run Segment or RudderStack. Everything after that is point-and-click.

What happens to user data and privacy?

Inputs and form fields are masked in the browser by default. You choose EU or US hosting, we sign a DPA on request, and you can delete any user's data with one API call.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing is charged unless you pick a plan. If you don't, your account pauses and keeps your data for 30 days in case you come back.
